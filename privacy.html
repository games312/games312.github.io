Block & Ball — Privacy Policy

Effective date / last updated: 2026-10-24

This Privacy Policy explains how the game “Block & Ball” for iOS and Android (the “Game”) handles information. The Game is published by 312 Games (“we”, “us”), who is the data controller for the Game. If you have any question, write to us at blockball.support@gmail.com.

1. The short version

  • You do not need an account, and there is no registration.
  • We do not collect your name, email address, contacts or photos, and we never ask for your location.
  • Your progress and settings stay on your device; we have no servers of our own. The Game uses Google Analytics for Firebase to learn how it is played (for example, levels played and purchases made) and in which country or region; no advertising identifier is used for this. See section 7.
  • The Game shows ads through Google AdMob. Google may collect some device data to show and measure ads. Personalised ads are shown only with your consent, and you can change your choice at any time.
  • Payments are handled by Apple or Google. We never see your payment details.

2. Information we do not collect

The Game has no account system. We do not ask for, collect or store your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos, files or precise or approximate location. We do not operate any servers that receive data from the Game.

3. Information stored on your device

To make the Game work, it saves your game progress (levels, stars, scores, items, gems), your settings (sound, language, theme and similar) and a record of your purchases and subscription status. This information is stored only on your device and in the app storage provided by your platform (Apple or Google), for example as part of your device backups. It is not sent to us, and we cannot access it.

4. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The Game shows ads using Google AdMob (the Google Mobile Ads SDK), a service provided by Google. When ads are loaded and shown, Google may collect and process the following information:

  • Your device’s advertising identifier: on iOS, the IDFA, but only if you allow tracking in Apple’s App Tracking Transparency prompt; on Android, the Android Advertising ID.
  • Your IP address, which may be used to estimate your approximate location (for example, your country or city).
  • Device and app information, such as device model, operating system version, language, screen size and app version.
  • Ad interactions, such as which ads were shown, viewed or tapped.
  • Crash and performance diagnostics related to the ads SDK.

Google uses this information to show ads, to limit how often you see the same ad (frequency capping), to measure ad performance, and to detect and prevent fraud and abuse.

Google may process this information in countries other than your own, under its own privacy policy and safeguards. To learn more, see:

  • Google Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy
  • How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

5. Your choices about ads and tracking

  • Personalised ads only with consent. If you are in the European Economic Area (EEA), the United Kingdom, Switzerland or certain US states, the Game asks for your consent using Google’s User Messaging Platform before personalised ads are shown. If you do not consent, you may still see non-personalised ads, which are based on general context rather than your profile.
  • Change your choice at any time. Open the Game’s Settings and tap “Ad privacy settings” to review or change your consent.
  • iOS tracking. On iOS, the IDFA is only available if you allow tracking. You can change this at any time in your device settings under Privacy & Security → Tracking.
  • Android advertising ID. On Android, you can reset or delete your advertising ID in your device settings (usually Settings → Privacy → Ads, or Settings → Google → Ads).
  • No feature is lost. You can decline tracking and personalised ads without losing any game feature.

6. Purchases and subscriptions

All in-app purchases and subscriptions (including VIP) are processed by the Apple App Store or Google Play. We never see or store your payment details, such as your card number or billing address. We receive only a confirmation from the store that a purchase was made, so that the Game can unlock what you bought. You can manage or cancel subscriptions in your App Store or Google Play account settings.

7. Analytics

The Game uses Google Analytics for Firebase to understand how it is played and to improve it. It collects app usage events (for example, levels played, features used and purchases made in the Game) and device information (for example, device model, operating system version, language and country). These are linked to a random app-instance identifier, not to your name or contact details, and the advertising identifier is not used for analytics. Google processes this data on our behalf under its own privacy policy; it is kept for up to 14 months.

8. Children

The Game is made for a general audience. It is not directed to children under 13 (or under 16 in the EEA). We do not knowingly collect personal data from children. Ads in the Game are set to a family-friendly content rating (“General” audiences). If you believe a child has provided personal data through the Game, please contact us at blockball.support@gmail.com and we will help.

9. Your rights

Depending on where you live, laws such as the EU and UK General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR / UK GDPR) and the California Consumer Privacy Act as amended by the California Privacy Rights Act (CCPA / CPRA) give you rights over your personal data, including the right to:

  • access the personal data held about you;
  • have your personal data deleted;
  • object to or restrict processing, including for personalised advertising;
  • withdraw your consent at any time (this does not affect processing done before you withdrew it);
  • lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority.

Because we do not hold any personal data on servers, most requests can be handled directly by you:

  • to withdraw or change ad consent, use “Ad privacy settings” in the Game’s Settings;
  • to delete all data stored by the Game, use “Reset progress” in Settings or delete the app;
  • for data processed by Google for advertising, use Google’s tools and settings described in Google’s Privacy Policy, and the advertising ID controls on your device.

We do not sell your personal information for money. For any request or question about your rights, contact us at blockball.support@gmail.com. We will respond within the time required by law.

10. Data retention

Data stored by the Game stays on your device until you delete the app or use “Reset progress” in Settings. Data collected by Google through AdMob is kept according to Google’s own retention policies.

11. Security

Your game data stays on your device and is protected by your device’s own security features. The ads SDK communicates with Google over encrypted connections. No method of storage or transmission is 100% secure, but because we have no servers, there is no database of player data on our side that could be exposed.

12. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time, for example when we add a new feature or service. When we do, we will change the “last updated” date at the top of this page. If the changes are significant, we will also let you know in the Game or in the store listing.

13. Contact

Data controller: 312 Games
Email: blockball.support@gmail.com

В Игре нет системы аккаунтов. Мы не запрашиваем, не собираем и не храним ваше имя, адрес электронной почты, номер телефона, контакты, фотографии, файлы, а также точное или приблизительное местоположение. У нас нет серверов, которые получали бы данные из Игры.

Чтобы Игра работала, она сохраняет ваш игровой прогресс (уровни, звёзды, рекорды, предметы, кристаллы), настройки (звук, язык, тема и т. п.), а также записи о покупках и статусе подписки.   • IP-адрес, по которому может определяться ваше приблизительное местоположение (например, страна или город).
  • Информацию об устройстве и приложении: модель устройства, версию операционной системы, язык, размер экрана, версию приложения.
  • Взаимодействие с рекламой: какие объявления были показаны, просмотрены или нажаты.
  • Диагностику сбоев и производительности, связанную с рекламным SDK.

Google использует эту информацию, чтобы показывать рекламу, ограничивать частоту показа одного и того же объявления, оценивать эффективность рекламы, а также выявлять и предотвращать мошенничество и злоупотребления.

Google может обрабатывать эту информацию за пределами вашей страны в соответствии со своей политикой конфиденциальности и мерами защиты. Подробнее:

  • Политика конфиденциальности Google: https://policies.google.com/privacy
  • Как Google использует информацию с сайтов и из приложений, которые используют сервисы Google: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites
